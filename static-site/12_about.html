Svantra
Since 2010 · India

Sampled Indian instruments — from India, for the entire world.

A sample-library studio built from the ground up to put the real sound of India into the hands of composers everywhere.

The culture.

Indian music is vast, regional, and mostly undocumented in the formats modern producers actually use. We sample it properly — at depth, with the masters who carry it — so it survives in the work of the next generation, not just in memory.

The people.

Every sound here was played by someone. We credit the artist, the recording director, and the collaborators by name — and we pay them. Not as a gesture. As the model. Contribution is compensated, morally and financially, the way a healthy creative economy should work.

The studio

It started with a room we built ourselves.

It began in 2010 with a stubborn idea and almost nothing else: that the instruments of India deserved to be deeply sampled — captured with their culture and history intact. We had no studio, no resources, and just a name: Crypto Cipher. So we started slowly, building the dream one step at a time — saving for the first microphones, finding the players, and raising a studio from scratch, tuned specifically for these instruments and the people who play them.

We record talented musicians from across the country — players of instruments that are rare, regional, and often impossible to track down outside India. The libraries go deep: the breath of a harmonium's bellows, the click of its keys, percussion patterns played the way the tradition intends. That depth is why the work travels — from bedroom producers to composers scoring for Hollywood.

The mission was never only software. It's local jobs, performances recorded at historical locations, and Indian classical music carried to a global audience ready to discover it.
